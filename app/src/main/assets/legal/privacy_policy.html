Privacy Policy

🃏Introduction

Welcome to Mindi & Mania: Color Card Game ("Mindi & Mania" or the "Game"), a free collection of card games developed and published by NGENDEV TECHNOLABS LLP (NG Studio), Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("we", "us" or "our"). The Game includes Mindi, Bhabhi, Spider Solitaire and the color card games Classic Mania, Merciless Mania and Flip Mania. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The short version: there is no account and no sign-up, we never ask for your real name, email or phone number, and your progress is saved only on your device. The Game has no ads and nothing is sold for real money. To find bugs and improve the Game, it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, and it can show you notifications; you can turn both off at any time in the Game's Settings. When you play with friends over Wi-Fi, the phones connect to each other directly on your local network.

By installing or playing Mindi & Mania, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

📦1. Information we collect

Information you give us

Only a player name and avatar of your choice, which can be any nickname. It is stored on your device and shown to other players only when you play with them over Wi-Fi. We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, location or any other personal information inside the Game.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the Game saves a small amount of data locally on your device only:

  • Your player name, avatar, level, experience points and coins
  • Your statistics for each game, achievements, daily challenge stars, Spider Solitaire records and the deals you played recently
  • The tables, card backs and avatar frames you have unlocked, and your daily gift streak
  • Games in progress, so you can continue later
  • Your settings, such as language, sound, music, vibration, game speed, accessibility options, notifications and data sharing
  • A random identifier created by the Game, used only to let you rejoin your seat in a Wi-Fi game if the connection drops

This data is not sent to us, except for the anonymous usage statistics described in Analytics, crash data and notifications. It is not linked to your identity.

Information collected by our partners

Google (through Google Play and Google Firebase) may automatically collect certain technical information, as described in Google Play and Android and Analytics, crash data and notifications below.

📱2. Device permissions

Mindi & Mania asks Android for these permissions:

  • Vibration, for the small buzz when cards are played or you win. You can turn it off with the Haptics switch in Settings.
  • Internet access and network state, to send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (if you allow it), to receive notifications, and to check for Game updates. Every game can be played offline.
  • Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi multicast and local network access, used only for Wi-Fi multiplayer: to find rooms on your network and connect the phones in a room. On newer Android versions you will be asked to allow the Game to find devices on your local network.
  • Notifications, for a daily reminder and for news about new games and events. On Android 13 and later the Game asks you first, and you can say no. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Game's Settings or in your Android settings.

The Game does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files, phone calls or messages.

📶3. Wi-Fi multiplayer

When you choose Multiplayer, phones on the same local Wi-Fi network connect directly to each other. The game is run by the host's phone; nothing passes through the internet or through us, and we do not store anything about multiplayer games.

  • Hosting a room makes it visible to other phones on that Wi-Fi network, showing the room and your player name.
  • The players in a room see each other's player name and avatar, the cards that are played and the emotes that are sent, just like at a real table. Each phone receives only what its player is allowed to see (for example, never another player's hand).
  • The random identifier described above lets you rejoin your seat if the connection drops. It is not linked to you or your device.
  • The connection ends when you leave the room or the host closes it.

Choose a nickname you are happy for the people on your Wi-Fi network to see. You can change it at any time in your profile.

🚫4. No ads, no purchases

Mindi & Mania does not show advertising and does not include any advertising software, so no advertising ID is used and no data is collected for ads.

Nothing in the Game is sold for real money. Coins, chests, tables, card backs and avatar frames are earned only by playing. There is no real-money gambling of any kind.

🔄5. Google Play and Android

Updates

The Game uses Google Play's in-app update service to check whether a newer version is available and, if you agree, to download and install it without leaving the Game. This is carried out by the Google Play Store app on your device under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Apart from the anonymous statistics described below (for example, that an update was offered), we do not receive any information about you from it.

Device backup

If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the Game's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled entirely by Google; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Google Play

When you download the Game, Google Play processes information such as your Google account and device details under Google's Privacy Policy. We may receive anonymous, aggregated statistics from Google Play (for example, the number of installs per country or crash counts) that do not identify you.

📊6. Analytics, crash data and notifications

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand which games are played and what to improve, the Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records anonymous events, such as screens opened, games started and finished (which game, whether you won, your finishing place, the number of players and whether it was a Wi-Fi game), daily gifts claimed, Wi-Fi rooms hosted or joined, your answer to the notification prompt, and update prompts. Each event comes with technical information: an app-instance ID (a random number created for this installation of the Game, not your name or advertising ID), device model, Android version, the Game's language and version, approximate location (country or region, worked out from your IP address) and the time of the event.

We do not use these statistics for advertising, and we never link them to your name, email or any account.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the Game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report so we can fix the problem. It contains technical details: what the Game was doing when it crashed (for example, the screen that was open and the Game's language), the device model, Android version, memory and storage state, the Game version and an installation ID (a random number for this installation of the Game).

Turning statistics and crash reports off

Both are on by default and can be turned off at any time with the Share usage & crash data switch in the Game's Settings. While it is off, no usage statistics or crash reports are sent.

Google processes this information for us as a service provider under the Firebase terms and Google's Privacy Policy, and keeps it only for a limited time (at most 14 months for usage statistics and 90 days for crash reports). Learn more at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you allow notifications, the Game can show at most one daily reminder, in the evening, about your daily gift or the daily challenge. It is created on your device, and nothing is sent to us to create it. Turn it off with the Daily reminder switch in Settings.

We may also send news about new games and events through Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives this installation of the Game a random token so that messages can reach it. Messages go to everyone, or to everyone using the same app language, never to a named person, and we do not link the token to you. Turn them off with the News & events switch in Settings (which also deletes the token) or in your Android settings.

Your device or Google Play may separately offer to share crash reports with developers. That sharing is controlled by your device settings and Google's own privacy policy.

⚙️7. How we use information

The limited information involved in Mindi & Mania is used only to:

  • Save your progress, coins, unlocked items, games in progress and settings so you can continue where you left off
  • Connect you with friends on the same Wi-Fi network when you play together
  • Find and fix crashes and bugs, and see which games and features need improving (anonymous statistics and crash reports, if you allow them)
  • Remind you about your daily gift or challenge, and tell you about new games and events (if you allow notifications)
  • Offer you updates to the Game
  • Reply to you if you contact us for support

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to build profiles about you.

🤝8. Data sharing

Your progress stays on your device. Information is shared only in these cases:

  • Google Firebase: the anonymous statistics, crash reports and notification token described in Analytics, crash data and notifications are processed by Google on our behalf.
  • Players on your Wi-Fi: your player name, avatar, played cards and emotes are shared with the players in a Wi-Fi room you host or join, as described in Wi-Fi multiplayer.
  • Legal reasons: if we are required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, ourselves or others.
  • Business transfers: if the Game is sold or transferred to another developer, this policy (or one at least as protective) will continue to apply.

🗑️9. Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Using Settings → Reset progress inside the Game, which erases your coins, levels, unlocks, statistics and achievements, or
  • Uninstalling Mindi & Mania, or clearing the app's storage in your device settings

Anonymous statistics and crash reports are kept by Google Firebase only for the limited time described in section 6. Data collected by Google Play is kept according to Google's own retention policies. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to help you, and then delete it.

🧸10. Children's privacy

Mindi & Mania is a card game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask anyone, of any age, for personal information.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law of their country. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at store.ngdtechnolab@gmail.com and we will delete it.

🛡️11. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally access. Wi-Fi games connect only phones on your own local network. We work only with reputable partners that use industry-standard safeguards.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but because we hold so little information, there is very little that could ever be exposed.

🎚️12. Your choices

  • Stop sharing statistics and crash reports: turn off Share usage & crash data in the Game's Settings.
  • Turn off notifications: use the Daily reminder and News & events switches in the Game's Settings, or your Android notification settings.
  • Play offline only: every game works without Wi-Fi multiplayer; you never have to host or join a room.
  • Change your nickname: edit your player name and avatar in your profile at any time.
  • Turn off vibration: use the Haptics switch in the Game's Settings.
  • Delete your data: use Reset progress in Settings, or uninstall the Game.

🌍13. Your rights around the world

You can play Mindi & Mania from anywhere. Information collected by Google Firebase may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards.

India

We follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You have the right to ask what personal data about you we hold, to have it corrected or erased, and to raise a grievance with us using the contact details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the Game stable and improving it; you can object at any time by turning off Share usage & crash data. Notifications are sent only if you allow them. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. We do not sell or share personal information, and the Game shows no ads. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Since we do not hold your game data on our servers and the statistics we receive are not linked to your identity, most requests are best handled by the in-game controls above. You are always welcome to contact us for help.
