Privacy Policy

Cards Mania · Effective date: November 5, 2026

◆●▲★

The short version

Cards Mania is a collection of card games you play against computer-controlled opponents, or with friends whose phones are on the same Wi-Fi network as yours. It is published by [Your Company] (“we”, “us”).

The collection includes Classic Mania, Merciless Mania and Flip Mania (color-matching games), Spider Solitaire (a single-player patience game) and Mindi (a team trick-taking game). This policy covers all of them in the same way.

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The game has no servers and never connects to the internet.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or registrations.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No internet connection is used: the game never talks to our servers or any third party. (Multiplayer uses only your local Wi-Fi network — see below.)
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Information kept on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of information only on your device:

  • The player name and avatar you choose (a nickname of your choice).
  • Game statistics, level, in-game coins, unlocked themes and achievements.
  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics, game speed, accessibility options) and any game in progress.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time with Settings › Reset progress, and it is removed automatically when you uninstall the app.

If your device’s own system backup feature is turned on, your operating system may include app data in your personal device backup. That backup is controlled by you and your device provider, not by us.

Local multiplayer on your Wi-Fi

When you choose Multiplayer, phones on the same local Wi-Fi network connect directly to each other. Nothing passes through the internet or through us.

  • Hosting a room makes it visible to other phones on that Wi-Fi network, showing your player name.
  • The players in a room see each other’s player name, avatar, the cards that are played, and emotes — just like at a real table.
  • A random identifier created by the app (not linked to you or your device) lets you rejoin your seat if the connection drops.
  • Nothing from a multiplayer game is stored by us; the connection ends when you leave the room.

Multiplayer works the same way in every game, including Mindi team rooms: the host’s phone runs the game and each phone only receives what its player is allowed to see. Team names and seats are shared only with the phones in that room.

On newer Android versions you will be asked to allow access to devices on your local network. This permission is used only to find rooms and connect the phones in a room.

Virtual coins and items

Coins, chests, themes, card backs and other items in Cards Mania are virtual. They are earned only by playing, have no monetary value, cannot be purchased with real money, and cannot be exchanged, transferred or redeemed for anything outside the game.

Children

Cards Mania is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided us personal information, please contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy (for example, if a future version adds a new feature), we will update the effective date above and include the new policy inside the app. If a change ever involved collecting data, we would tell you clearly in the app before it applied.
