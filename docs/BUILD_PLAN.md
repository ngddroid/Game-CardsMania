## 0. One-paragraph summary

**Cards Mania** is an original, offline, color-matching shedding card game for Android with three modes:
**Classic Mania**, **Chaos Mania** and **Eclipse Mania**. They cover the same kind of play as a classic game, a
"no-mercy" stacking game and a two-sided "flip" game, but use **our own brand, card names, card art, gem suits, UI,
sounds and rule text**. You play against 1–5 bots (2–6 players total), and you choose the mode, the bots, their
difficulty, the starting hand size, the deck size and the house rules. All graphics are drawn in code (Compose
Canvas, gradients, shaders and particles), so there are no third-party art assets and no licensing risk.

## 2. Game rules: full analysis of the three modes

### 2.1 Shared core rules (all modes)

1. **Setup.** Shuffle and deal `H` cards to each player (default 7; you can choose 5/7/10). Turn the top card of the
   draw pile face up to start the discard pile.
2. **Matching.** On your turn, play one card that matches the top discard by **color**, by **value** (number) or by
   **symbol** (same action type), or play a **Prism** (wild).
3. **Can't play.** Draw 1 card; if it is playable you may play it immediately, otherwise your turn ends. (Optional
   house rule: *Draw to Match*, where you keep drawing until you get a playable card. This is always on in Chaos.)
4. **Direction.** Play starts clockwise from the dealer's left. A Reverse flips the direction.
   **With 2 players, Reverse acts as Block.**
5. **MANIA! call.** When you play your second-to-last card, you must call **MANIA!**. If you don't, any other player can
   **Catch** you before the next player acts, and you draw **2** penalty cards. Bots sometimes forget, depending on
   difficulty, and you can catch them.
6. **Running out of cards.** When the draw pile is empty, keep the top discard, shuffle the rest into a new draw pile and
   reset Prism colors. If there are still too few cards, draw as many as exist. If the draw and discard piles are both
   exhausted and nobody can play, the round ends and the lowest hand total wins (this deadlock guard is our own rule).
7. **Round end.** The first player to empty their hand wins the round. (In Chaos, the last player standing also wins.)
8. **Scoring (optional; the default is a single round).** The round winner scores the sum of the cards left in the
   opponents' hands. The first player to reach the target score wins the match (100 / 250 / 500 / 1000).
9. **Dealer.** The first dealer is chosen at random; after that the dealer role rotates clockwise.
10. **Turn timer (optional).** 10 s, 15 s, 30 s or off. When time runs out, the game auto-draws for you.

### 2.2 Classic Mania — 108 cards

**Deck composition**

| Card | Per suit | Suits | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 4 | 4 |
| 1–9 | 2 each (18) | 4 | 72 |
| Block | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| Reverse | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| +2 | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| Prism | — | — | 4 |
| Prism +4 | — | — | 4 |
| **Total** | | | **108** |

**Card effects**
- **Block**: the next player loses their turn.
- **Reverse**: the direction flips (with 2 players, it acts as Block).
- **+2**: the next player draws 2 and loses their turn.
- **Prism**: choose the next color. You can play it at any time.
- **Prism +4**: choose a color; the next player draws 4 and loses their turn. It is only *legal* when you hold no card
  of the current color.
  **Challenge:** the victim may challenge. If the play was illegal, the player who played it draws 4 instead. If it was
  legal, the challenger draws 6. (Default on; can be turned off.)

**Effect of the first flipped card:** Prism +4 goes back into the pile and the pile is reshuffled. Prism lets the first
player choose the color. Block skips the first player. Reverse flips the direction, so the dealer plays first. +2
makes the first player draw 2 and skips them.

**Points:** a number card scores its face value. Block, Reverse and +2 score 20. Prism and Prism +4 score 50. The default
target score is 500.

**Optional house rules** (toggles on the Setup screen):

| Rule | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Stacking | +2 on +2, and +4 on +4 (or on +2). The accumulated total passes to the next player. |
| Jump-In | If you hold an *identical* card (same color and value), play it out of turn. Play continues from you. |
| Seven-Zero | Playing a 7 swaps your hand with a player you choose. Playing a 0 passes every hand in the direction of play. |
| Draw to Match | Keep drawing until you get a playable card. |
| Forced Play | A playable drawn card must be played. |
| Combo Drop *(our addition)* | Play every card of the same number at once (for example, three 5s of any suits). The last one played sets the color. |
| Mania Power-ups *(our addition)* | Playing action cards fills a meter. When it is full, use **Peek** (see the top 3 draw cards) or **Shield** (block one incoming draw penalty), once each per round. |

### 2.3 Chaos Mania — 168 cards (stacking, swaps, elimination)

**Deck composition (our own)**

| Card | Per suit | Total |
|---|---|---|
| 0–9 | 2 each (20) | 80 |
| Block | 3 | 12 |
| Reverse | 3 | 12 |
| +2 | 3 | 12 |
| +4 (colored) | 2 | 8 |
| Purge | 3 | 12 |
| Block All | 2 | 8 |
| Prism Rebound +4 | — | 8 |
| Prism +6 | — | 4 |
| Prism +10 | — | 4 |
| Color Storm | — | 8 |
| **Total** | | **168** |

**Rules (always on in this mode)**
- **Draw to Match.** If you can't play, keep drawing until you can. You may then play the drawn card.
- **Stacking.** Any draw card can be stacked on a draw card of **equal or higher** value: +2 → +2/+4/+6/+10, +4 → +4/+6/+10,
  and so on. Colored draw cards must still match by color or by symbol. Prism draw cards can always be stacked.
  **Prism Rebound +4** flips the direction **and** adds 4, so the stack moves to the next player in the new direction.
  A player who can't (or won't) stack draws the whole total, and their turn ends.
- **Seven:** swap hands with any player you choose. **Zero:** every player passes their hand in the direction of play.
- **Purge:** play it, then also discard every other card of that color from your hand. Purge stays on top and the
  color stays the same.
- **Block All:** every other player is skipped and you play again.
- **Color Storm:** the *next* player names a color, then reveals cards from the draw pile until that color appears.
  They keep every revealed card and their turn ends.
- **Overload rule:** a player holding **25 or more cards** is eliminated immediately. Their cards are shuffled into the
  draw pile. If only one player is left, that player wins the round.
- **Prism draw cards have no "only if you can't match" restriction** in this mode.
- **First flipped card:** if it is any Prism, it goes back into the pile and the pile is reshuffled. Other action cards
  apply to the first player.

**Points:** number cards score face value. Block, Reverse, +2 and Purge score 20. Block All and +4 score 30. Prism
Rebound +4 and Color Storm score 50. Prism +6 scores 60. Prism +10 scores 100. The default target score is 1000; the
default match length is a single round.

**Edge cases we handle explicitly**
- An overload that happens in the middle of a stack, while the cards are being drawn, eliminates the player right away.
  The stack is consumed and does not pass on.
- With 2 players, Block All works the same as Block.
- A Seven swap can make a player hit 25 cards. Overload is checked after every hand change.
- A Zero rotation that includes eliminated players skips their seats.
- Color Storm when the chosen color no longer exists in the draw pile: reshuffle the discards and continue. If the color
  doesn't exist anywhere, stop once the piles are exhausted.

### 2.4 Eclipse Mania — 112 two-sided cards

Every card has a **Sun face** and a **Moon face**. Everyone plays on the active side. **You can see the *other* side of
your opponents' cards, but not the other side of your own.** That hidden information is the core strategy of this mode.

**Sun side (bright suits: Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Amber)**

|---|---|---|
| 1–9 | 2 each (18) | 72 |
| +1 | 2 | 8 |
| Reverse | 2 | 8 |
| Block | 2 | 8 |
| Eclipse | 2 | 8 |
| Prism | — | 4 |
| Prism +2 | — | 4 |
| **Total** | | **112** |

**Moon side (night suits: Rose, Aqua, Tangerine, Violet)**

| Card | Per suit | Total |
|---|---|---|

| +5 | 2 | 8 |
| Reverse | 2 | 8 |
| Block All | 2 | 8 |
| Eclipse | 2 | 8 |
| Prism | — | 4 |
| Prism Hunt | — | 4 |

**Our twist:** in each match, Sun faces and Moon faces are **paired randomly** when the deck is built. A physical deck
has its pairs fixed when printed; ours change every match.

**Eclipse card:** the whole game flips to the other side. All hands, the draw pile (which is turned over, so its order
reverses) and the discard pile change faces. The Eclipse card stays on top and shows its other face, which sets the new
color and value. **That face's action is not triggered.** If it is a Prism, the player who played Eclipse picks the color.
- **+1 / +5:** the next player draws 1 or 5 and loses their turn.
- **Block All:** everyone else is skipped and you play again.
- **Prism +2:** choose a color; the next player draws 2 and loses their turn.
- **Prism Hunt:** choose a color; the next player draws until they reveal that color, and their turn ends.
- The Prism-draw challenge from Classic is an optional toggle (default off).
- Play starts on the **Sun** side.

**Points:**

| Side | Card | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | Number | face value |
| Sun | +1 | 10 |
| Sun | Reverse, Block, Eclipse | 20 |
| Sun | Prism | 40 |
| Sun | Prism +2 | 50 |
| Moon | Number | face value |
| Moon | +5, Reverse, Eclipse | 20 |
| Moon | Block All | 30 |
| Moon | Prism | 40 |
| Moon | Prism Hunt | 60 |

Cards are scored on the side that is active when the round ends. The default target score is 500.

### 2.5 Game Setup screen (the user decides everything)

1. **Mode**: Classic, Chaos or Eclipse. Large animated mode cards.
2. **Players**: 2–6 total; you plus 1–5 bots. For each bot, pick a character and a difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard or
   **Genius**. You can also pick "All bots: Hard", or similar, in one tap.
3. **Starting hand**: 5, 7 or 10 cards.
4. **Deck size**: Standard or **Double deck** (recommended for 5–6 players in Chaos).
5. **Match length**: Single round, or a target score (100/250/500/1000).
6. **House rules**: the mode-specific toggles from §2.2–2.4.
7. **Game speed**: Relaxed, Normal or Fast (scales animation and bot "thinking" time).
8. A **Quick Play** button on Home skips setup and uses your last settings.

## 3. Bot AI (four difficulty levels)

| Level | Brain |
|---|---|
| **Easy** | Plays a random legal card and picks a random color. Forgets MANIA 35% of the time. Slow to catch you. |
| **Normal** | Uses heuristics. Picks the color it holds most of. Saves Prisms for later. Plays action cards against a next player who has 2 or fewer cards. Sheds high-value cards when an opponent is close to winning. Forgets MANIA 10% of the time. |
| **Hard** | Everything Normal does, plus **card counting** (it tracks played cards), **void tracking** (if a player drew on a color, it assumes they lack that color) and stack planning. It uses a Seven swap on the smallest hand and evaluates whether a Zero rotation helps it. It times Eclipse plays by reading opponents' visible other sides. It never forgets MANIA and catches instantly. |
| **Genius** *(our addition)* | Uses **ISMCTS** (information-set Monte Carlo tree search). It samples the hidden hands consistently with what it has seen, then runs 200–600 rollouts within a ~250 ms budget on a background thread. |

Bots have **personalities**: 12 original characters (Captain Ace, Lady Luck, Ziggy, Momo, Rex, Nova, Bolt, Pixel, Sage,
Kiki, Bruno, Lumi). Each has a gem-framed avatar, a playing style bias (aggressive, hoarder, chaotic and so on) and
**emote reactions** (laugh, angry, wow, GG) shown as animated bubbles. Bot think time is 0.6–1.4 s with natural jitter,
scaled by game speed.

## 5. Graphics: the Playground (where most of the effort goes)

Everything is drawn in Compose. Each card face is **rendered once into a cached `ImageBitmap`**, so animations only move
bitmaps and stay at 60 fps even on mid-range phones.

**Table**
- Layered felt: a radial gradient, a procedural noise texture (generated once to a bitmap), a vignette, and soft light
  pools.
- Ambient particles: slow floating gold dust; sparks in Chaos mode; stars in Eclipse Moon mode.
- Center: a discard pile showing the last 5 cards with random ±12° rotation and offsets, and a draw pile with real 3D
  depth (stacked edges and a shadow).
- **Direction ring**: an animated glowing ring around the piles. Its arrows run in the play direction, it is tinted the
  current color, and it spins the other way with a spring "whip" on Reverse.
- **Color aura**: the table glow cross-fades to the current color whenever the color changes.
- Theme packs: Royal Velvet (default), Emerald Casino Felt, Neon Night, Ocean Deep, Desert Sunset and Cosmic. They
  change the gradient, particles and ring style.

**Seats** (2–6, arranged on an ellipse around the top of the screen)
- Avatar in a gem frame with a **turn-timer ring** that drains around it, plus name, card-count badge and a fanned mini
  hand.
- The active player gets a pulsing halo and a slight scale-up.
- **Eclipse mode:** opponents' mini hands show the card faces on their *other* side, which is the strategic information.
- Overloaded players get a crack-and-shatter animation, then their seat greys out.

**Your hand**
- An arc fan at the bottom. When the hand grows, the overlap squeezes; past 14 cards it becomes a scrollable arc.
- Playable cards lift by 12 dp with a gem glow; unplayable cards dim to 70%.
- Tap to select (the card lifts), then tap again or **flick up** to play. Drag follows your finger with tilt that depends
  on velocity.
- Buttons: **Sort** (by color or by value, with animated re-ordering), **Hint** (the best card pulses), and **MANIA!**
  (appears at 2 cards, pulses and has a shine sweep).

**Card design** (5:7 ratio)
- Rounded corners (8% radius) and a thin gold outer frame.
- The body has a gem-colored diagonal gradient with a faceted sheen and an inner bevel stroke.
- The center emblem is a **faceted rhombus** (not an oval) holding a large value or action icon with a drop shadow.
- Corner indices show the value and the **suit symbol** (Ruby flame, Sapphire droplet, Emerald leaf, Amber star). The
  Moon suits have their own symbols.
- Prism cards have a four-color faceted gem, with an animated holographic shimmer shader on API 33+ (`RuntimeShader`)
  and a static gradient as the fallback.
- Card backs are dark navy with a gold **CM** monogram and a starburst guilloché pattern. Back designs are
  collectible.

**Animations** (all spring- or easing-based, scaled by game speed, and replaced with simpler fades when Reduce motion is on)

| Event | Animation |
|---|---|
| Deal | Cards fly from the deck along curved Bézier arcs to each seat, staggered 60 ms, with a spin and a whoosh per card |
| Play card | Arc flight from the hand to the pile with a 3D flip (rotationY plus camera distance), a landing squash and a dust puff |
| Draw | The card slides off the deck, flips face-up only for you, and settles into the hand |
| Block / Block All | A shield icon slams onto the target seat(s) and the avatar shakes |
| Reverse | The ring whips the other way and an arrow trail orbits the table |
| +N / stacking | A counter badge grows on the pile ("+8!") with a heat-glow color ramp. Big hits shake the screen and send a card torrent to the victim |
| Prism color pick | A radial gem picker blooms from the card, and the chosen color floods outward over the table |
| Color Storm | Lightning flashes, then cards are revealed one by one with a rising pitch until the target color hits |
| Seven swap / Zero rotate | The hands lift and fly between seats along crossing arcs |
| Purge | The matching cards flare and burn away in a particle trail into the pile |
| **Eclipse** | A full-screen sun/moon transit. The table palette morphs from day to night, every card on screen flips in a ripple wave, and the music cross-fades to its night variant |
| MANIA! | A gold shockwave with a big type pop and a camera punch |
| Catch | Red "CAUGHT!" stamp and two cards fly to the offender |
| Win round | Confetti burst, the winner's avatar crowned, and remaining hands fly to the scoreboard as tallied points |
| Match win | Full-screen trophy, a fireworks particle system, a coins and XP counter roll, and unlock reveal cards |

**Original addition — "Big Play" replay toast:** after a huge stack or a dramatic Eclipse, a small slow-motion
highlight banner plays for 1.2 s, for example *"Lumi stacked +18 on you!"*.

## 6. App icon, splash and brand

- **Icon:** an adaptive vector icon. Three fanned gem cards (Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald) with a gold "CM" monogram gem in
  front, on a royal-purple radial burst. It has a monochrome layer (Android 13 themed icons) and a round variant. A

  Tangerine `#FF7A1A`, Violet `#8B5CF6`.

## 7. Extra features beyond the reference

1. Gem suits with symbols: color-blind friendly by design.
2. **Genius bot** (ISMCTS) plus 12 bot personalities with emotes.
3. **Daily Challenge**: one seeded deal per day with a fixed mode and rules, 3-star goals, and a streak counter.
4. **Gift Chest**: a daily reward every 24 h (coins, a cosmetic shard). No real money, no gambling mechanics.
5. **Collection**: 6 table themes, 10 card backs and 8 avatar frames, unlocked with coins earned from play or with levels.
6. **Achievements (30)** and **weekly missions**.
7. **Stats dashboard** per mode, plus match history for the last 20 matches.
8. **House rules**, including our own Combo Drop and Mania Power-ups (Peek and Shield).
9. **Resume game**: the match is saved automatically when the app goes to the background.
10. **Interactive How to Play** with animated demos for every card.
11. Big Play highlight toasts, haptics on every important event, and a left-handed layout option.
12. Double-deck option for large tables.

**Key design choice:** the engine emits a **list of `GameEvent`s** for every action (CardPlayed, CardsDrawn,
DirectionChanged, ColorChosen, HandsSwapped, PlayerEliminated, SideFlipped and so on). The UI's
**AnimationDirector** plays those events in order as animations and sounds, then unlocks input. As a result the rules
stay 100% testable and never depend on UI timing.

**Dependencies to add:** navigation-compose, lifecycle-viewmodel-compose, datastore-preferences, core-splashscreen,
kotlinx-serialization-json (plus the Kotlin serialization plugin), kotlinx-coroutines and material-icons-extended.
**No** ads, analytics or network libraries. The manifest declares **no** INTERNET permission.

**Orientation:** portrait-locked. It plays well with one hand and suits a 6-seat ellipse layout.

## 9. Sound design

All audio is generated by `tools/gen_audio.py`, which uses only the Python standard library (`wave`, `math`, `random`);
numpy and ffmpeg are not installed on this Mac. It writes 16-bit WAV files into `res/raw`.

- **SFX (~22):** card slide, card snap (3 pitch variants), deal whoosh, shuffle riffle, draw, button tap,
  toggle, error buzz, Block clang, Reverse swoosh, +N thump (deepens with stack size), stack-rising tick, Prism
  chime arpeggio, Color Storm thunder, Eclipse "whoomp" plus shimmer, MANIA! sting, Catch stamp, overload
  shatter, coin clink, chest open, win fanfare and lose sting.
- **Music (3 loops, ~30–45 s each, seamless):** a menu theme, a Day table theme and a Night (Eclipse Moon) variant of
  the same progression, so the cross-fade sounds musical. Each is synthesized from layered square, triangle and noise
  voices with simple ADSR envelopes and reverb-like delay.
- Runtime: SoundPool with random ±4% pitch variation so repeated plays don't sound robotic; ducking on big events;
  volumes from Settings.

> Honest note: code-synthesized audio will sound clean and "arcade-polished", but not like a studio-produced score.
> The `audio/` layer is file-name based, so you can later drop in professionally licensed files (for example a CC0 pack
> such as Kenney's) without any code changes.
