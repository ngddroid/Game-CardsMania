## How Play search ranks you (what this listing is built on)

* Play reads the **title** most, then the **short description**, then the **long description**. There is no separate
  keyword field. Release notes and tags are not used for search text.
* Put the words players type first: **Mindi, Bhabhi, Spider Solitaire, card game, offline**. Repeat each a few times
  in the long description in natural sentences (stuffing or keyword lists get the listing rejected or ranked lower).
* Never use another company's game name or brand (title, descriptions, screenshots or tags).
* After launch, ranking depends mostly on **installs, rating and players who keep playing**. Check
  *Play Console → Grow users → Store performance → Search terms* after 2–4 weeks and move the words that bring
  installs higher in the short description.
* Add the **Hindi and Gujarati translations** below as their own listings (Store listing → Manage translations →
  Add your own translations): people searching in Devanagari or Gujarati script find you, and few rivals do this.

Play Mindi, Bhabhi, Spider Solitaire and three fast color card games in one app. Mindi & Mania is a desi card game collection you can play offline against smart bots, or with friends and family on the same Wi-Fi – no internet and no sign-up needed.

♦ MINDI (MENDIKOT / MINDI COAT / DEHLA PAKAD)
The classic Indian team card game. Play with 4, 6 or 8 players in two teams (one or two decks), pick how the trump (hukum) is chosen – hidden, cut or open – and win the tens (mindis) with your partner. Take every ten for a Mendikot, or every trick for a whitewash. Play a single hand or a full match.

♠ BHABHI (THULLA / GET AWAY)
The famous shedding card game for 3 to 8 players. Follow suit, and when you can't, give a thulla and make the highest card pick up the trick! Empty your hand to get away – the last player holding cards is the Bhabhi. No hints: play a wrong thulla while you still hold the suit and you take 5 extra cards as a penalty.

♥ SPIDER SOLITAIRE
Relaxing solitaire with 1, 2 or 4 suits. Every 2-suit and 4-suit deal is checked by our solver: hard, but always winnable. Daily Spider challenge, unlimited undo, hints when you ask for them, and auto-finish.

## Privacy Policy and Terms

The pages are in `app/src/main/assets/legal/`, made like the Balls Adventure ones (same sections, company details,
"Powered by NG Studio" footer), in this game's purple and gold:

* `privacy_policy.html`, `terms_conditions.html`, and `index.html` (website home with the Play button)
* `legal.css`, `app_logo.png`, `ng_studio_logo.webp`, `fonts/` (Lilita One + its license)

The app shows them from this folder. Play needs the Privacy Policy at a **public web address**, so upload the whole
folder as a website, for example with **Firebase Hosting** like Balls Adventure (`game-balladventure.web.app`):

1. In the Firebase console, add a Hosting site (for example `mindi-mania`), then deploy the folder
   `app/src/main/assets/legal` as its public directory.
2. Play Console → **Policy → App content → Privacy policy**: `https://<your-site>.web.app/privacy_policy.html`
3. Terms & Conditions: `https://<your-site>.web.app/terms_conditions.html` (Play has no field for terms; link it
   from the website home).
4. After changing a page, redeploy so the website and the app stay the same.
